Every layer is its own group (Inkscape & LightBurn layers) and all text is converted to outlines, so no fonts are needed. Color per layer lets you set a different speed/power for each layer in LightBurn. The insert-pieces sheet has one matching shape per visible icon, ready to cut from a second board.
